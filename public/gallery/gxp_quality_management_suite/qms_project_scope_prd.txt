# Quality Management Platform (QMS) - PRD & Project Scope

## Overview
A GxP-compliant platform consolidating quality processes across manufacturing sites.

## Core Screens to Generate
1. **Executive Quality Dashboard**: High-level overview of 27+ processes, NCR trends, audit status, and site performance.
2. **Document Control Center (SOPs & Work Instructions)**: Lifecycle management for SOPs, versioning, and e-signature status.
3. **CAPA & NCR Management**: Tracking corrective/preventive actions and non-conformance reports with workflow stages.
4. **Internal Audit Hub**: Scheduling, checklists, and real-time reporting for manufacturing site audits.
5. **Supplier Quality Scorecards**: Performance tracking and quality metrics for external vendors.

## Key Constraints
- GxP compliance requirements (Audit trails visible)
- E-signature integration points
- Multi-site navigation
- High data density requirements for industrial use cases
